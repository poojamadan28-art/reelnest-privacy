ReelNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

ReelNest ("the app") is published by Swarna Utility Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

We do not collect, store or share your personal data ourselves. ReelNest has no accounts, no cloud servers and no analytics. Everything you save stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads. See "Advertising" below.

Information stored on your device

When you save a video, the app stores the link, title, caption, hashtags, creator name, thumbnail link, your notes and your categories in a database on your phone. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share or export it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Internet access

When you save a link, the app connects directly from your phone to the site the link points to (for example YouTube, TikTok or Instagram) to read the video's public title, caption and thumbnail. Only the link you chose to save is sent, and only to that site. These sites handle the request under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or log these requests.

Sharing and export

When you share a reel or export a category or backup file, the app hands the content to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or email) using Android's share menu. Who receives it is entirely up to you.

Advertising

ReelNest shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner at the top of some screens and, now and then, a full-screen ad after you finish an action such as saving a reel. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app collects:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location, based on your IP address
  • how you interact with the ads (views and taps)
  • diagnostic data such as app crashes and performance

This data goes to Google, not to us. Google uses it as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. Your saved reels, categories and notes are never shared with Google or any ad network.

Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy settings in the app's menu. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Ads in ReelNest are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Feedback and ratings

If you tap Send a suggestion, the app opens your own email app with a new message addressed to us. The message includes the app's version number and your phone's operating system version, to help us fix problems. You can read and change everything in the message before sending it, and nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. We use emails you send only to reply to you and to improve ReelNest. We don't share them with anyone.

If you tap Rate on Google Play, the app opens ReelNest's page in the Google Play Store. Ratings and reviews you leave there are handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

The app counts how many times it has been opened, so it knows when to show the rating prompt, and remembers whether you want to see the welcome guide. Both are stored only on your phone.

Children

ReelNest is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children, and ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Permissions

  • Internet: to read the title and thumbnail of links you save, and to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (added by Google AdMob): to show and measure ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
