ReelNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

ReelNest ("the app") is published by Swarna Utility Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

We do not collect, store or share any of your personal data. ReelNest has no accounts, no cloud servers, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you save stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

When you save a video, the app stores the link, title, caption, hashtags, creator name, thumbnail link, your notes and your categories in a database on your phone. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share or export it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Internet access

When you save a link, the app connects directly from your phone to the site the link points to (for example YouTube, TikTok or Instagram) to read the video's public title, caption and thumbnail. Only the link you chose to save is sent, and only to that site. These sites handle the request under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or log these requests.

Sharing and export

When you share a reel or export a category or backup file, the app hands the content to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or email) using Android's share menu. Who receives it is entirely up to you.

Children

ReelNest is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Permissions

  • Internet: to read the title and thumbnail of links you save.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
